I am a cell biology PhD with research training in cancer biology, tumor metastasis,
immunity, and post-translational regulation. My current work is moving toward
computational biology, with a focus on single-cell and spatial omics, cancer
bioinformatics, and practical AI4Science workflows.

I care about questions where biological mechanism, reliable data analysis, and
modern machine learning can meet without losing scientific grounding.

## Research Focus

- **Single-cell and spatial omics**: cellular heterogeneity, tissue context,
  tumor microenvironment, and reproducible analysis workflows.
- **Cancer bioinformatics**: public cohort mining, multi-omics integration,
  metastasis and immune regulation, and hypothesis generation from omics data.
- **AI4Science for biology**: foundation models, representation learning, AI
  pathology, target discovery, and protein or molecule-centered modeling.
